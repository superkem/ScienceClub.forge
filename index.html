What is Element Forge?

A science-club crafting game. Gather raw materials (ores, water, air, crude oil…), drop them in the pot, pick the right machine and discover what you can make — from iron and glass to ammonia, solar cells and rockets, plus a few crafts you might know from Minecraft or Roblox.

Stuck? Win mini-games in the Puzzle Lab to earn 💡 hints, recipe scrolls and materials. Mine carefully: digging pollutes 🌍!

1. Gather – click ⛏ on a material to collect more.
2. Mix – click materials to fill the pot (up to 6, amounts matter!).
3. Cook – choose a machine. Wrong try? Nothing is lost.
4. Solve – puzzles unlock hints for the Codex 📖.
